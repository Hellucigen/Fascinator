## 2. Relevance to the journal

The journal's scope explicitly includes cognitive modeling, the design and
experimental evaluation of agent architectures, and embodied artificial
intelligence. This paper contributes on exactly those axes: (i) an
auditable unified-graph cognitive architecture (FAS) in which episodic,
semantic and procedural knowledge share one structure with a single
evidence-weighted write-back pathway; (ii) preregistered, fully archived
behavioral experiments in a deterministic embodied sandbox — including a
preregistered rejection of the architecture's own headline hypothesis
(spreading activation showed no incremental value over flat retrieval);
and (iii) a boundary analysis, with parameter sweeps, of when
activation-based access does and does not pay. JETAI has historically
published the cognitive-architecture line this work builds on (Soar,
ACT-R-inspired designs) and current embodied-agent research; the
methodology (preregistration + reproducibility package) is offered as a
contribution to the experimental standards of that community.
